**Death by Scone (A Maple Hollow Bakery Mystery #3)** by Penny Ashcombe
★★★☆☆ (3.5, rounding down because of the cat thing, see below)

Okay so I flew through this in like two evenings, which is exactly what I want from a cozy. Hattie Pruitt is back running her little bakery in Maple Hollow, and this time the grumpy food critic who trashed her lemon scones in the local paper turns up dead behind the library. Guess who's the main suspect? Yep. Our girl.

What I liked: the town is SO charming. I want to live there. The knitting circle ladies are hilarious, especially Dot, who I'm convinced is secretly a retired spy. The recipes at the back are a nice bonus (I made the cardamom buns and they were legit good). The actual mystery had a decent twist. I had it narrowed to two people and picked the wrong one, so points for that.

My gripes:

1. The love triangle. PLEASE. Detective Cole or Ben the hunky orchard guy, just pick one, Hattie. Three books in and I'm exhausted.

2. Biscuit the cat "finding" a crucial clue by knocking it off a shelf. I know it's a cozy, I know. But it happened TWICE. Once is cute. Twice is lazy plotting.

3. Hattie keeps doing dangerous stuff alone at night after literally everyone tells her not to. Girl, text somebody.

Still, I'll absolutely read book four. It's comfort food in book form. Just maybe give Biscuit a break next time.
