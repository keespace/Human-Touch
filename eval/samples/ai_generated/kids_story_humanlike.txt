**The Mitten Who Went to the Bus Stop**

Once upon a time there was a red mitten named Gerald. (You named him that. I wanted to call him Mittsy, but you said Mittsy was "a cat's name," which, okay, fair.)

Gerald lived on your left hand. He had a little hole on the thumb where you chewed it that one time at Grandma's, and he smelled a tiny bit like peanut butter crackers.

One Tuesday, Gerald fell off at the bus stop. Nobody noticed. Not you, not me, not even Mrs. Patterson's dog, Biscuit, who notices EVERYTHING, including invisible squirrels.

So Gerald just lay there in the slush. He said, "Well, this is boring."

A pigeon came by and looked at him. Gerald said, "Hello." The pigeon said nothing because pigeons are rude. (They are. Remember the one that took your french fry?)

Then it started to snow. Gerald got a little snow hat. He thought he looked pretty fancy, actually.

Then a big orange snowplow went BRRRRRMMM right past him, and Gerald held on to the curb with his thumb, the one with the hole, and said, "NOT TODAY, SNOWPLOW."

And then guess who came walking back, holding a flashlight and saying "Where is it, where is it, where is it"?

Us! In our pajama pants! With Daddy's big boots on the wrong feet!

You picked Gerald up and squeezed him and said, "You're SOGGY." And Gerald said, "I had an adventure."

And then we went home and put him on the heater, where he is right now. Snoring.

Goodnight, bug.
