**So I'm Learning to Drive. At 40. Yes, Really.**

Okay so this is happening. I've got my learner's permit and I'm officially the oldest person in the DMV line by about twenty-two years. The kid in front of me was wearing Crocs with those little charms in them and honestly I was jealous, my feet were killing me.

Anyway. People keep asking *why now* and the truth is I don't totally know. I lived in the city forever, took the train everywhere, and then we moved out here where the nearest grocery store is a twelve minute drive and there's no bus. So. Here we are.

My instructor's name is Dale. Dale is very calm. Dale has clearly seen things. The first lesson I put the car in reverse instead of drive and he just said "okay" in this voice like a hostage negotiator. I think about that "okay" a lot.

Things I didn't expect:
- How LOUD everything is? Like the turn signal clicking made me so anxious
- Parallel parking is a hoax invented by people who hate me
- Other drivers can tell. I don't know how but they can tell

Side note, I've started noticing cars now in a way I never did before. Like I used to think all cars were basically "blue one" or "big one." Now I'm like oh that's a Subaru, those people seem confident. I don't know why Subaru drivers seem confident to me. They just do.

My sister (who's been driving since she was 16 and won't let me forget it) offered to take me out on weekends. We went once. We're not doing that again, I'll leave it there.

I've got my test schedueld for March. Dale says I'm "getting there." I asked him where exactly and he didn't really answer, just told me to check my mirrors.

I did not check my mirrors.
